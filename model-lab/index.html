GRIM WORLD MODEL LAB

Each page is a standalone turntable for one rig. It cycles that rig's states on a five second timer and exposes a __shot hook so the harness can freeze any state at any point in its cycle. A rig gets proven here before it is wired into the game, because a rig that ships broken means every monster using it ships broken.

  • QUAD
    wolf. Shipped. The reference for four legged beasts.
  • SERPENT
    Ten link parented chain driven by one wave equation. Bog serpent, and CONSTRICT.
  • WISP
    Core, halo, orbiting motes and a light. Ice sprites, will-o-wisps, dust devils.
  • FLYER
    Three joint wings that fold on the upstroke and perch. Cave bats, vultures.
  • CRAB
    Eight legs on a travelling side strafe, two jointed claws. PINCER GRAB.
  • INSECT
    Six legs on the alternating tripod gait, optional tail. Rock crawlers, scorpions, BURROW AMBUSH.

Shared scene, loft, textures and turntable live in lab.js. wolf.html predates it and keeps its own copy, deliberately: it is the shipped reference and nothing should be able to change it by accident.
