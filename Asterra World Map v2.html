Waterways and choke points

1Great River · navigable by barge from Lake Argent to the Suncoast delta
2Cinder Run · navigable from the Mistfen marshes to Fenmouth
3Headwater streams in the mountains are NOT navigable; those regions trade by road, which is what makes Highpass, Frost Gate, and Ember Gap matter
4The Narrows · the only safe lane through the Shattered Isles; all cross-sea trade funnels here
5Argent Bridge and Kingsford Bridge · the two road crossings of the Great River
